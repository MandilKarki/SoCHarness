iPhone → Mac → another laptop

  1. Sign in with your operator token on your iPhone, then choose Add passkey.
  2. Approve the system prompt with Face ID. Save to iCloud Keychain to sync with your Mac.
  3. On another laptop, choose a passkey from a nearby device, scan its QR code with your iPhone, and approve. Keep Bluetooth on.

An up-to-date browser and device are required. Relay cannot enroll a device for you. You can also start enrollment on a laptop and choose your nearby phone.

Keep a recovery route

Your operator token still works if you lose access to your devices. Keep a secure copy outside this browser.

Removing a passkey signs out every Relay browser session. It does not delete the credential from Apple Passwords; you can remove that copy yourself.

This pilot has no team accounts, role permissions, or email recovery. Server restarts require another sign-in; registered passkeys persist on the encrypted volume.

Remove this passkey?

Every browser session will be signed out. Make sure you have another passkey or your operator recovery token.
